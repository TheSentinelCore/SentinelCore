# How To Get Gold

## Overview

Sylvanas uses **Gold** as its in-game currency. You acquire Gold by purchasing it, then spend it on:

- **Base Subscriptions** – Required to play with Sylvanas for a selected duration.
- **Add-On Services** – Options like Phone HWID (mobile linking) or premium plugins from the marketplace.

This guide covers:

1. How the Gold system works
2. Purchasing Gold (officially and via resellers)
3. Redeeming codes from resellers
4. Spending Gold on subscriptions and services

## 1. How the Gold System Works

1. **Buy Gold** – Acquire Gold units in your account.
2. **Spend Gold** – Navigate to the appropriate panel page to purchase:
   - **Subscriptions**: Unlock Sylvanas for a period.
   - **Phone HWID**: Link your Sylvanas license to your phone for portable use.
   - **Plugins**: Enable or purchase additional scripting features.
3. **Manage Balance** – Any leftover Gold remains in your wallet for future purchases.

Gold is priced on the official site at **$1 USD = 1,000 Gold**. Example:

- To buy a 1-month subscription (29,700 Gold), pay **$30 USD** and keep the remaining 300 Gold in your balance.

## 2. Purchasing Gold

You can obtain Gold in two ways:

### A. Official Website (BTC & LTC Only)

1. Go to the [Buy Gold page](https://project-sylvanas.net/panel/shop/coins).
2. Select **Bitcoin (BTC)** or **Litecoin (LTC)** as your payment method.
3. Complete the crypto transaction.
4. Gold is credited automatically to your Sylvanas account.

> **Note**
> 
> Only BTC and LTC are accepted here. If you need other payment options, see **Resellers** below.

### B. External Resellers (PayPal, Credit Card, CashApp, etc.)

On the same Buy Gold page, choose **"PayPal, CC & More (Resellers)"**. A pop-up will list available vendors. Each offers different Gold packages, prices, and payment methods.

Below are some commonly used resellers:

- **Shop:** [Mew Shop](https://apmnerds.com/)
- **Process:** Mew now hosts an automated shop for purchases.
- **Supported payments:** Google Pay and standard credit card payments.
- **Note:** This is the fastest and easiest purchase method.

#### Mew, PayPal

- **Process:** Open a ticket on our [Discord](https://discord.gg/SDe3ze5bPx) and tag **@mew_am**. Mention that you would like to purchase via PayPal, and all payment instructions will be provided in the ticket.
- **Note:** This is a manual process, so it may take longer than purchasing through the auto shop.

#### Travers – Discord

- **Shop:** [Travers Shop](https://whyspdigital.com/products/wow-sylvanas)
- **Contact:** [Travers Discord](https://discord.gg/A9dkkmZwNu)
- **Process:** Travers shop webpage, instant delivery once payment is received.

#### Sylvanas_CN – 支付宝 / 微信 (Alipay / WeChat Pay)

- **商店:** [sylvanas.cc](http://www.sylvanas.cc/)
- **如何购买:** 选购商品并填写邮箱，下单后兑换码会发送至您的邮箱，用于在官网兑换金币。

#### Weavers – PayPal / Crypto

- **Shop:** [weaverxstore.com](https://weaverxstore.com/)
- **Process:** Provide your Discord ID & email; receive code by email after payment.

> **Tip**
> 
> Choose the vendor that best fits your preferred payment method and regional convenience.

## 3. Redeeming Your Code

If you purchased through a reseller, you will receive a **Redeem Code**. To add this Gold to your account:

1. Go to the [Buy Gold page](https://project-sylvanas.net/panel/shop/coins).
2. At the top, find **Enter Redeem Code**.
3. Paste your code and click **Apply Code**.
4. Your Gold balance will be updated immediately.

## 4. Spending Your Gold

Once you have Gold in your account, choose how to spend it:

### Base Subscription

- Link: [Subscription Panel](https://project-sylvanas.net/panel/shop/subscription)
- Use Gold to buy monthly or multi-month access to Sylvanas.

### Phone HWID (Mobile Linking)

- Link: [Phone HWID Panel](https://project-sylvanas.net/panel/shop/hwid)
- Enables your Sylvanas license on any PC via your phone. Ideal for internet cafés or travel.

### Plugin Marketplace

- Link: [Plugin Marketplace](https://project-sylvanas.net/panel/plugins/marketplace)
- Browse free and paid plugins.
- **Free Plugins** still require a 0-Gold purchase to add to your **Plugin Library**: [Plugin Library](https://project-sylvanas.net/panel/plugins/library).
- After purchase, enable or disable plugins in your library as needed.
