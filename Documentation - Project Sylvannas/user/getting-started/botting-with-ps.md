# Botting with PS

Project Sylvanas (PS) is designed to **assist you while you play**, not to fully take over gameplay.

Because of that, **botting with PS is not recommended by default**.

That said, we offer an optional shop add-on called **Botting Bypass**.

With Botting Bypass, unattended gameplay becomes possible, but it is still a **risk** and you are still responsible for how you use it.

> **Important**
> 
> Botting can lead to bans.
> 
> Botting Bypass does **not** make you unbannable, it only exists for users who choose to bot anyway.

---

## What is Botting Bypass

**Botting Bypass** is a paid PS add-on that sits on top of the base PS subscription.

It is:

- **Optional**, you do not need it to play with PS
- **Not required**, most users should not buy it
- **Not a safety upgrade**, it does not make PS "safer" in general
- **Only relevant** if you intend to bot with PS

Botting Bypass does not give you new features for normal assisted play.

It does not unlock special plugins.

It does not change what exists in the Marketplace.

It only matters if you are going to run long unattended sessions.

> **Keep in mind**
> 
> As a rule of thumb, any gameplay longer than **5 minutes** without your inputs is considered **botting**.

## What Botting Bypass is not

Botting Bypass is not a ban shield.

Botting Bypass does not make you immune to bans.

Botting Bypass does not remove the need to be responsible with session length, behavior, and frequency.

If you bot, you are still subject to bans for bot-like patterns, long play sessions, repetition, and other signals.

## Responsible session guidelines

If you choose to bot, be realistic about risk.

These guidelines exist to reduce obvious "suicide botting" patterns:

- **Longer than 4 to 6 hours** in one session is never recommended
- **More than 12 hours** inside a 24 hour cycle is considered **suicide botting**
- Doing very long sessions **more than once a week** dramatically increases risk

Even without perfect detection, extremely long continuous play is a common reason for bans.

> **Risky behavior**
> 
> If you repeatedly run 12 plus hour cycles, you can be banned simply due to session length patterns.

## Eligibility and access

To use PS normally, you need an active **PS subscription**.

Botting Bypass is an additional subscription add-on.

Without Botting Bypass, you should treat PS as an assisted play tool and stay active at the keyboard.

## Marketplace and bot oriented plugins

Botting Bypass does not create new plugins.

It does not unlock more Marketplace content.

If there is no Marketplace plugin that fulfills your botting needs, you should not buy Botting Bypass.

Inside the Marketplace UI, you can filter to show **botting oriented** plugins, if any exist for your use case.

## How to bot with PS

We cannot provide step by step instructions for unattended botting or guidance focused on bypassing detection.

We can provide safe guidance instead:

- Only use PS as an assist tool while you are actively playing, unless you accept the risk of botting
- Avoid long continuous sessions and repetitive schedules
- Prefer short, variable sessions, with real breaks

## Summary

- PS is built to assist active play
- Botting is not recommended by default
- Botting Bypass is optional, paid, and only relevant if you will bot
- Botting Bypass does not make you safer in general, and does not make you unbannable
- Extremely long sessions, especially 12 plus hours in a 24 hour window, are a high risk
- Buy Botting Bypass only if the Marketplace already has a plugin that matches your needs
